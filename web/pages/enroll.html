Enroll this device

What this protects, and what it does not. Your key is created in this browser and never leaves it; the site stores only its public half. But:

  • a script injected into this site while your key is unlocked could read it, or copy the sealed key and try to guess your passphrase offline — the guessing is deliberately slow, but a weak passphrase still loses, which is why short and common ones are refused;
  • there is no hardware protecting it, as a passkey has;
  • your browser may clear this site's storage, and a copy of it would be an undetectable clone of this device.

Keep your recovery codes (from the Account page) somewhere safe.

Enroll this device

What this protects, and what it does not. Your key is created in this browser and never leaves it; the site stores only its public half. But:

  • a script injected into this site when you type your passphrase can read the passphrase, and with the sealed key this browser keeps, that is your key. The page clears the passphrase the moment it is used, but it cannot stop a script that is already running;
  • a copy of the sealed key taken without your passphrase can be guessed at offline — the guessing is deliberately slow, but a weak passphrase still loses, which is why short and common ones are refused;
  • there is no hardware protecting it, as a passkey has;
  • your browser may clear this site's storage, and a copy of it would be an undetectable clone of this device.

Keep your recovery codes (from the Account page) somewhere safe.
